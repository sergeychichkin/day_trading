Run Details

Revised adds the Entry 2 doji and VWAP conditions and waits for a bullish candle before moving the stop. Entry 3, when enabled, uses the same RID breakout setup in both rule versions. Both versions support order replacement and sequential trades.

Times are New York minute labels. Signal and stop updates use completed candles. ATR(20) uses one-minute true ranges and carries across regular sessions. Trade PnL already includes fill slippage and commissions. Slippage includes gaps through a stop trigger.

IBKR Pro Fixed broker commission is modeled at $0.005/share per order with a $1 minimum and 1% trade-value cap. Additional regulatory and venue fees are excluded. The buy-and-hold comparison uses unleveraged price change and excludes dividends and trading costs.

ATR/Close uses the previous completed session's Wilder daily ATR divided by that session's close. MFE measures the favorable price move from the entry fill and excludes unsequenced exit-minute highs. Daily ATR is blank during warmup.
