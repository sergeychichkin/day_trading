Run Details

Each replay starts flat with configured cash. Earlier sessions supply indicator warmup and prior daily levels. Strategy returns and the benchmark exclude dividends. The replay uses stored prices and models no corporate-action adjustments to positions or orders.

IBKR Pro Fixed uses $0.005 per share per executed order, a $1 minimum and a 1% trade-value cap. Venue and regulatory fees are excluded. Net P&L already reflects opening gaps, adverse slippage and commissions; the cost columns are not additional deductions. “Adverse gap vs. trigger/stop” measures entry overshoot above the trigger and exit shortfall below the protective stop at an opening print. Other overnight price movement is included in P&L. The benchmark excludes trading costs.
