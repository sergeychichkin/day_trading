Run Details

IBKR Pro Fixed uses $0.005 per share per executed order, a $1 minimum and a 1% trade-value cap. Venue and regulatory fees are excluded. Net P&L already reflects opening gaps, adverse slippage and commissions; the cost columns are not additional deductions. “Adverse gap vs. trigger/stop” measures entry overshoot above the trigger and exit shortfall below the protective stop at an opening print. Other overnight movement is included in P&L, but not in this column. The benchmark excludes trading costs and dividends.
